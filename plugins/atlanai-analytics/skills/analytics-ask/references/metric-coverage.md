# Describe search coverage accurately

Search results are ranked matches, not a complete workspace inventory. Describe them as the
closest published metrics found. Do not turn a limited result set into a claim that the
workspace has only those metrics, or that a missing hit proves no definition exists. When a
relevant definition was not found, say that. Claim a complete list or total only after a
workspace-scoped listing with all pages, or an authoritative count with the same scope.

Keep the measure, release, workspace and any inventory filters explicit. A workspace total
does not establish that an unrelated metric answers the requested measure. Do not add an
inventory scan to every numerical question; qualify the search results when that is enough.

**Nothing found** is an answer too: say no matching published definition was found for the measure,
then continue with your own SQL and its assumptions spelled out (step 5 labels it).
